# Design

## Look

- White background, black text, gray accent `#6b7280` (secondary text, accents).
- Buttons: solid black, robust.
- Radius: 2px.
- No borders.
- Minimal Shadows.
- Plain background.
- Font: League Spartan.
- Icons: thin `react-icons`.
- Avoid custom CSS; prefer Tailwind utilities.

## Voice

- Minimal wording.
