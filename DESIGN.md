# Design

## Look

- White background, black text, gray accent.
- Light only.
- Buttons: solid black, robust.
- Radius: 2px.
- No borders.
- Minimal shadows: one subtle shadow.
- Plain background for now; a touch may be added later.
- Font: League Spartan.
- Icons: thin `react-icons`.
- Avoid custom CSS; prefer Tailwind utilities.

## Voice

- Minimal wording.
- English only.
